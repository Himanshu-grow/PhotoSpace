01 / THE IDEA

Why I made PhotoSpace

Photographs are more than images. They hold places we've been, people we've met, and little details that time has a habit of quietly taking away.

I wanted one calm, personal place to keep my photographs organised, add context to them, and make it easy to revisit them later. No feed to keep up with, no numbers to chase. Just my photos, their stories, and the freedom to keep adding to the collection.

02 / THE PERSON BEHIND IT

Hi, I'm Himanshu Sharma.

I'm a student, a curious observer, and someone who enjoys learning by making things. PhotoSpace brings together two interests of mine: photography and building things on the web.

This project is a work in progress, made for personal use and improved one small detail at a time. The idea is simple: make a home for the photographs I take and the memories I want to keep close.
