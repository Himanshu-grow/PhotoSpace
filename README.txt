**PhotoSpace** 📸

A personal photography-sharing platform built to celebrate moments, creativity, and visual storytelling.

PhotoSpace provides a clean, intuitive space to upload, organize, explore, and share photographs. Designed with a minimalist interface, it puts the focus where it belongs: on the pictures and the stories behind them.

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, IndexedDB, and Supabase.

**Every picture has a story. Give yours a space.** ✨
